# Leadership

| Style                        | Definition                                                                 | Traits                                       | Pros                                                    | Cons                                                    | Best Used When                                                |
|------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Autocratic**               | Leader makes decisions unilaterally, with little input from team members. | Directive, top-down, control-focused         | Quick decision-making, clear roles                     | Low morale, limited creativity                          | Urgent situations, military, high-risk environments            |
| **Democratic (Participative)**| Leader involves team in decision-making                                    | Inclusive, collaborative, input-driven       | High engagement, better morale, diverse ideas          | Slower decisions, potential conflicts                  | Creative work, skilled teams, problem-solving                  |
| **Transformational**         | Inspires and motivates employees to exceed expectations.                   | Visionary, charismatic, change-oriented       | Drives innovation, motivates employees                | May overlook details, risk of burnout                  | Leading change, innovation, cultural shifts                  |
| **Transactional**            | Focuses on structured tasks, rewards, and penalties.                       | Task-oriented, performance-based              | Clear structure, efficient for routine work            | Limited innovation, low engagement                    | Process-driven environments, production lines                |
| **Laissez-Faire**            | Hands-off approach with high employee autonomy.                            | Trusting, non-interfering, empowering         | Fosters innovation and independence                    | Lack of direction, chaotic with inexperience          | Skilled, self-motivated teams                               |
| **Servant**                  | Prioritizes the needs of the team and their growth.                        | Empathetic, supportive, ethical               | High trust, strong team cohesion                       | Slow decisions, risk of being exploited                | Building team culture, mentoring roles                      |
| **Coaching**                 | Focuses on personal development of team members.                           | Mentoring, feedback-oriented, supportive      | Individual growth, long-term performance improvement  | Time-consuming, less effective with unmotivated staff | Developing future leaders, training-focused environments |
| **Bureaucratic**             | Strict adherence to rules and procedures.                                  | Rule-following, hierarchical, cautious        | High consistency, strong compliance                   | Inflexible, slow to adapt                             | Government, finance, regulated industries                  |
